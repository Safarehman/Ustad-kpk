# KPK Textbooks (KPTBB) — Class 9 & 10

Downloaded 2026-10-21 from awazeinqilab.com (Google Drive links on each book page).
That archive is the source behind the "KPK Books and Notes" app; the official
KPTBB site (tbb.kp.gov.pk) has no direct book downloads.
Class9-Biology-Urdu.pdf recovered 2026-10-22 from taleem360.com
(cdn1.taleem360.com, 291 pages, 54.4 MB, SHA-256 verified) — the awazeinqilab
copy was deleted at source. Also carries a "perfect24u" watermark.

**Verification done:** every file below is a valid PDF (checked header), page
counts are sane, and sample pages were visually checked — genuine KPTBB books
with the government "NOT FOR SALE" marking (e.g. Class 9 Physics Unit 1,
Class 10 Chemistry Ch. Chemical Equilibrium).

**IMPORTANT for the app build:** these are SCANNED IMAGE PDFs with no text
layer. An AI tutor cannot read them as-is — they need OCR first
(e.g. ocrmypdf/Tesseract, or an AI vision pass page-by-page) before the
content can be used for grounding/RAG. Some books also carry the archive's
"awaz e inqilab" watermark/footer on pages.

Total: 33 books, ~1.2 GB.
